Sizes and sources

Work zone signs (tagged 6F-1) use MUTCD 2009 Table 6F-1, which the ODOT 2009 Traffic Control Standards follow. Their Expressway and Freeway sizes come from the table's shared freeway/expressway column. Other signs come from Table 2B-1 (regulatory), 2C-2 (warning), 7B-1 (school) and 9B-1 (bicycle), plus a few route markers from 2D-2. Multi-lane, Expressway, Freeway, Minimum and Oversized sizes are used where the tables list them. If a size is missing, the closest available size is used and marked. Signs whose width varies aren't listed; add those as project-specific signs.

How area is calculated

Diamonds are side × side (36×36 = 9.00 SF). Octagon = 0.8284·W². Equilateral triangle = 0.433·S². Circle = π·D²/4. Pentagon (school) ≈ 0.75·W·H. Crossbuck = 2·L·W − W². Rectangles = W × H, with rounded corners ignored. The W × H basis uses width × height for every shape instead.

Pay ranges

A less than 6.25 SF · B 6.25–15.99 SF · C 16–32 SF. Exactly 6.25 SF is B and exactly 16.00 SF is C. Signs over 32 SF are listed separately.

Warning lights (MUTCD §6F.83)

All four types are portable, powered, yellow, lens-directed lights, with the bottom of the lens at least 30 in above the ground. In this tool, barricades and flag trees start with one Type B light each. You can change the light type or remove it on any line.

Channelizing devices and barricades (§6F.63–6F.69)

Cones: 18 in minimum for daytime low-speed work, 28 in minimum for high-speed or night work. Drums: at least 36 in tall and 18 in wide. Barricade rails: 8–12 in wide. Type I and II rails are at least 24 in long and Type III rails at least 48 in. Direction indicator barricades: a 24×12 in W1-6 arrow over a 24×8 in rail. Arrow boards: Type A 48×24, Type B 60×30, Type C 96×48, and Type D vehicle-mounted.

Limits

The sign pictures are simplified drawings. The ODOT standard sheets are scanned, so sizes weren't checked against them sheet by sheet. Sizes marked "verify" come from memory of the MUTCD tables. The project plans, ODOT standards and special provisions govern.
